The Problem

Ticketing platforms tell you who bought a seat. They cannot tell you who sat in it. For a venue like FedEx Field, the gap between tickets sold and fans in seats directly impacts concession revenue, sponsor activation value, atmosphere, and broadcast product quality — yet no existing tool measures this in real time at the section level.

How It Works

Our methodology captures live secondary market seat availability at two points per game:

  • Pre-game — 60 min before kickoff, baseline by section
  • Mid-game — halftime, seats back on the market

The delta between these snapshots identifies confirmed no-show seats — mapped by section, price tier, opponent, and day of week. Fully automated. No manual reporting.

What We Deliver for the Commanders

  • Per-section no-show rates every home game
  • Opponent & day-of-week attendance breakdowns
  • Secondary market pricing trends pre- & in-game
  • Live dashboard, auto-updated after every game
  • Season-over-season trend analysis
