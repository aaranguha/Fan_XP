What Fan XP does

Fan XP identifies seats that were listed for resale before a game and never got resold or used by kickoff — a confirmed no-show. We let fans claim those seats for the second half, subject to the seat owner's confirmation.

How a claim works

  • You submit a bid for a specific seat. Your card is authorized, not charged.
  • We text the seat owner asking them to confirm the release.
  • If they confirm, your card is charged and you receive a gate pass code.
  • If they decline, or don't respond within the offer window, the authorization is released and you are never charged.

We do not guarantee that any specific seat owner will respond or confirm. A claim is a request, not a purchase, until it is confirmed.

Payments

Payments are processed by Stripe. By submitting a claim, you agree to Stripe's terms in addition to these. Fan XP does not store your card details.

Your responsibilities

  • Provide accurate name and phone number when claiming a seat.
  • Show your gate pass code and valid ID if asked, when entering the venue.
  • Don't attempt to claim a seat you don't intend to use.
