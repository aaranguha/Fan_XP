Privacy Policy

Last updated August 28, 2026

Fan XP is an early-stage product. This policy describes our actual current data practices in plain language, but has not been reviewed by a lawyer. It will be replaced with formal legal terms before any public launch.

What we collect

When you claim a seat, we collect the name and phone number you provide, and the seat (team, section, row, seat number, price) you're claiming.

We never see or store your card details. Payment is handled entirely by Stripe — we only receive confirmation that a charge was authorized or completed.

How we use it

  • To text the seat owner asking them to confirm the release, and to text you the outcome.
  • To process payment authorization and capture through Stripe.
  • To show you the status of your claim when you check back on the page.

Who we share it with

  • Stripe — processes your payment. See Stripe's privacy policy.
  • Twilio — delivers the SMS messages referenced above.
  • Supabase — hosts the database where claim records are stored.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Cookies and tracking

This site does not currently use analytics, tracking pixels, or advertising cookies.

Your choices

To request that we delete your data, email us using the contact below. Because seat claims involve a real financial transaction, we may need to retain records required for payment disputes or tax purposes even after a deletion request.

Contact

Questions about this policy: reach out via the waitlist form on our homepage.
